About the app

My Health Pal is a patient-facing mobile app for iPhone and Android. It lets a person connect each hospital, health system or practice that holds their records, then shows those records in one place:

  • Labs and test results, combined across health systems into one history per test, with each result's reference range and any flag the reporting lab included.
  • Health summary: conditions on the problem list, medications, and allergies.
  • Visits and procedures as one timeline.
  • Exports of the combined records (PDF, JSON, CSV) that the person can save or share themselves.
  • Access history: a complete log of every time the app connected to, retrieved records from, or disconnected a health system, and every export.

Every record is labeled with the health system it came from.

On first install, the app shows sample data from two synthetic health networks ("Riverside Health (demo)" and "Lakeview Medical Group (demo)") with records for a fictional patient, so people can try every feature before connecting real accounts. The sample data is labeled on every screen and can be removed at any time.

No medical advice. The app only connects to existing services and displays what they return. It does not interpret results, diagnose, or recommend treatment. Questions about your health or your records should go to your care team.

How it works

  1. You search for a health system in the app and choose it.
  2. The app opens that health system's own sign-in page. You sign in with your usual patient-portal login (for example, MyChart) and approve read-only access. The app never sees or stores your password.
  3. The health system gives the app a temporary access token, using the industry-standard SMART on FHIR protocol (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE). The app uses it to read your records through the health system's patient-access API.
  4. You can repeat this for each health system, and disconnect any of them at any time.

Access is read-only. The app cannot change your records or act in your patient portal. Access tokens expire after a short time; to update your records later, you sign in again.

This website also hosts the short page the app returns to after sign-in. It passes the sign-in result back to the app on your phone and stores nothing.

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Where your data is stored

Your records are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure keychain (iPhone) or keystore (Android). The app has no servers of its own and does not upload your records anywhere.

How long your data is kept

Your records stay on your phone until you disconnect that health system, use "Disconnect all and delete data," or uninstall the app. Nothing is deleted automatically, and nothing is kept anywhere else.

Who your data is shared with

No one. The app communicates only with the health systems you choose to connect. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and does not sell, rent or share your information. Your data is never used for any purpose other than showing it to you.

Exports

If you export your records, the file goes wherever you choose in your phone's share sheet (for example, your files, email, or another app). What happens to it after that is up to you and that destination.

Your control

  • Get a complete copy of everything the app holds about you at any time: "Health systems" tab → Export (PDF, JSON or CSV).
  • See who accessed your data: the "Access history" section of the "Health systems" tab lists every time the app retrieved your records (date and time, health system, record types) and every export. The same log is included in the PDF and JSON exports. No one other than you, including the app's developer, can access your records.
  • Disconnect a health system in the app's "Health systems" tab. The app revokes its access where the health system supports it and deletes that system's records from your phone.
  • Delete everything with "Disconnect all and delete data," or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can also revoke the app's access from your health system's patient portal.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing their own records.
